### Sun Nov 29: This label's math is off (new video)

This protein bar says 190 calories. The math says 226.

4 x protein + 4 x carbs + 9 x fat = 226. But fiber counts about half and erythritol counts zero, which brings it to 198. Labels round every value, so a few percent off is normal.

Numbers: the bar's label and FDA labeling rules (21 CFR 101.9).

### Tue Dec 1: Do you need 1 g of protein per pound? (new video)

Do you really need 1 g of protein per pound?

In a review of 49 trials, extra muscle gain from protein leveled off near 0.7 g per lb a day. 1 g per lb is the top of the range.

A 150 lb person: about 105 g a day.

Source: Morton et al., 2018, British Journal of Sports Medicine.
