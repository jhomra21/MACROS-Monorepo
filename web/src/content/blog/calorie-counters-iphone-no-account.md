## Where might another app fit better?

The app pages describe different priorities. Cronometer publishes detailed nutrient tracking. MyNetDiary lists a large verified food catalog. Foodnoms supports Mac and Apple Watch. MacroFactor offers adaptive targets. MACROS does not support Android or iOS 25 and earlier. FatSecret is not included because its current first-party platform and feature details have not been checked for this draft.

These are features described by the companies, not results from a head-to-head test. We have not tested the apps against one another, compared their privacy labels, or verified every regional price and plan.

## What should someone know before choosing MACROS?

MACROS requires iOS 26 or later. Anyone using iOS 25 or earlier, or looking for an Android app, should choose an app that supports their device. MACROS also uses public food databases for network lookups, and those lookups need an internet connection.
