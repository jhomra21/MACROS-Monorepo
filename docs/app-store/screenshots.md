# App Store screenshot plan

Capture after Phase 2 is merged and the final release build is available. Use the same seven story beats for the 6.9-inch and 6.5-inch iPhone display sizes, with separate correctly sized exports. Keep copy legible and make sure screenshots match the submitted binary.

1. **Onboarding calculator** — Show a clear, editable goal estimate; caption: “Start with goals that fit you.”
2. **Dashboard** — Show a realistic logged day and macro rings; caption: “See your day at a glance.”
3. **Add food** — Show search and quick-entry options; caption: “Log food your way.”
4. **Barcode and label scan** — Show packaged-food scanning and a reviewable result; caption: “Scan, review, and log.”
5. **Insights** — Show Pro nutrition charts without implying medical recommendations; caption: “Spot your nutrition trends.”
6. **Sharing circle** — Show only aggregate totals and invite controls, with sample data; caption: “Share daily totals with your people.”
7. **Pro paywall** — Show monthly, yearly trial, and lifetime options accurately; caption: “More ways to understand your progress.”

Use synthetic sample names and nutrition data. Do not expose real user information, imply iCloud sync, AI logging, or Apple Watch support, or present features that are not in the release binary.
